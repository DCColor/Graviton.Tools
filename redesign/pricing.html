How licensing works

Two ways to buy

Graviton apps are offered under two licensing models. Desktop apps are sold as one-time perpetual licenses. Scope, our web-based platform, is a subscription. Every product includes a free trial so you can evaluate it before paying.

Perpetual (desktop apps)

Pay once and own that version. Activate on up to 4 machines per license. Point releases (e.g. 1.1, 1.2) are free for the life of your license. Major versions (e.g. 2.0) may be offered as a paid upgrade — this is not guaranteed and will be decided at the time of release.

Subscription (Scope)

Billed monthly or annually. Includes all updates while your subscription is active. Cancel any time — access continues through the end of your current billing period.

Payments are securely processed by Paddle.com, our Merchant of Record. Paddle handles checkout, applicable sales tax and VAT, and appears on your statement. See our Refund Policy for details on returns, and our Terms of Service for the full license agreement.
